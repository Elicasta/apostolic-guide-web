## Editorial workflow

1. Check the **2021 EKGE** wording for each cited verse (exact chapter, verse, and context).
2. Compare our Ekegusii explanation with the approved English explanation. Preserve negations, names, the difference between divine identity and Jesus' genuine humanity, baptism, repentance, salvation by grace, and distinctions between historical narrative and doctrinal interpretation.
3. Mark **Bible wording checked** only after comparison. Keep a separate **Ekegusii translation approved** status, only for a native-speaker reviewer.
4. Submit the corrected text through the Pathway's **Suggest corrections** form. It does not automatically publish.
